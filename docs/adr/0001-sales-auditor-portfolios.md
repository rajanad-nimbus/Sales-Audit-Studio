# Sales Auditor portfolios

ReSA associates audit employees with locations through location traits. ZeTSA therefore scopes the Sales Auditor business role (`analyst`) to an audited local store assignment of explicit stores and/or live Foundation location traits, and fails closed when no assignment exists; identity-provider store claims do not widen that portfolio. Internal Auditors retain enterprise-wide read access for independent review, and are not treated as Sales Auditors for portfolio assignment.
